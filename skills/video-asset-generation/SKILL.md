# Video asset generation

Identify the narrative or visual gap, intended timestamp, duration, camera perspective, palette, motion, and audio role before generating an asset. Reuse suitable supplied assets first. Choose an available image, video, audio, Blender, or graphics tool based on the needed output; do not assume a generator is installed.

Generate only assets that support the user's brief. Check continuity of character identity, framing, lighting, and pacing. Place B-roll where it covers or enriches a deliberate transition, and keep an audit trail of prompts, source files, timing, and usage rights where relevant.

Preview the asset in the actual composite. Trim or regenerate if it creates a jarring visual or audio change. For reference recreation, avoid adding music, B-roll, or effects that alter the original staging unless the user requests them.
